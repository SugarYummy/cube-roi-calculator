# Cube ROI Calculator

Website: https://sugaryummy.github.io/cube-roi-calculator/

Shows which item gives you the most Final Damage per cube.

## How to use
1. Type your Maple Scouter values (Stat Efficiency, Detail Eff.).
2. Pick your gear and its 3 lines.
3. Cube the item at the top of the results first.

Your setup saves in your browser. Use **Copy link** to share your gear with friends.

Cube rates from [brendonmay's cubing calculator](https://brendonmay.github.io/cubingCalculator/).
