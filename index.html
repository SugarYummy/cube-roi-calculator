    How it works
    • Odds: Bright Cube uses Black Cube odds and Glowing Cube uses Red Cube odds, Legendary only, from brendonmay's cubing calculator.
    • Your FD: comes from your own Maple Scouter numbers (Detail Eff.). The FD of each potential is added together, which is great for comparing but not exact. IED uses Boss PDR 380%.
    • Ranking: for each item it checks targets like "33%+ Magic ATT" and picks the one with the most FD per cube (or the fewest cubes). All Stats counts toward your stat %. When a target shows a Choices box, hitting any one of those mixes (or more) counts.
    • Auto-reroll targets: settings for the in-game Target presets. One is only suggested if everything it stops on is an upgrade for you. The game adds up totals, and All Stats counts toward your main stat.
    • Mesos: Heroic world prices. Bright 22M and Glowing 12M each, plus the meso fee each cube use charges (20 × item level²). The 25% off Cash Shop cube sale turns on by itself on sale days.
    • Before & After image: in step 2, click Save current gear before you cube (keep as many saves as you like). Make image compares any save with another save or your current gear, ready to copy into Discord.
    • Budget plan: type your mesos (Mesos mode) or your cubes (Cubes mode). It cubes each item in list order until it hits the target, 4,000 times, and shows how far you get most of the time. Turn it off with the Budget plan box above the list.
    • Your data: saved in your browser only. Copy link puts your gear in the link.
    • Not exact for: Demon Avenger, Xenon, and Cooldown Hat values from Maple Scouter.
    • Found a bug or a wrong number? Report it on GitHub.
    FD gain = how much Final Damage you gain.
    FD per X cubes = average FD you gain for every X cubes you use. Higher is better.
    Cubes per 1% FD = the average price of 1% Final Damage, in cubes. Lower is better. It's a price to compare items, so the item doesn't need to give a full 1%: +0.5% at 1 in 400 costs 800 cubes per 1% FD.
    Mesos = Heroic world prices (Bright Cube 22,000,000, Glowing Cube 12,000,000 each, 25% off in Cash Shop cube sales) plus the meso fee each cube use charges: 20 × item level² (Lv 200 = 800,000).

    Where to find Stat Efficiency

    On your Maple Scouter page, it's in the left column under the Scouter Graph. Click Detail Eff. and copy the rows with arrows.

    Where to get Cooldown Hat values

    In Maple Scouter, turn on Additional Spec Simulation. Under Input, type the seconds in Cooldown Reduction, click Apply, then copy the FD % under Boss 380. Do this for each number of seconds you want.

    Type 2, not -2. Maple Scouter already counts it as a reduction, so a minus sign shows lost damage.

    Type your mesos spent to see your luck. It compares your mesos to what a typical player spends to reach these results (half spend less, half spend more). Exact lines counts the lines you got, or anything worth more FD (an All Stats line counts like the same % of your main stat, so 12 / 12 / 9 All Stats counts as 12 / 12 / 9).
