Your gear

All items count as Legendary. Ring 4 (continuous ring) and Pocket are left out because they aren't cubed.

How it works: every possible 3-line result is checked with its real chance. "FD gain" is the smallest gain you get when you hit the target. "FD per 1k cubes" = FD gain × chance × 1,000, so a longer bar means more Final Damage per cube. Bright Cube uses Black Cube odds and Glowing Cube uses Red Cube odds, from Nexon's published rates (data via brendonmay's cubing calculator). Lv 160+ items get +1% on Stat, All Stats and ATT lines.
